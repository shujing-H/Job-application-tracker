PRIVACY POLICY

Your job search data stays yours.

Effective date: August 26, 2026

What the extension stores

When you visit a supported job-detail page, the extension may store a local draft containing the company, role, location, job URL, source, and job-description snapshot. Drafts are kept on your device for up to seven days unless you extend or delete them.

What is sent to Google Sheets

Only records you explicitly confirm as an application or referral request are sent to the Google Sheet you connect. A confirmed record may include the company, role, location, date, source, initial status, current status, referral flag, job URL, job-description snapshot, and notes.

Google account access

The extension uses Chrome's Google identity flow and the narrow Google Drive drive.file scope. This permits access to sheets created or previously authorized by the extension, not arbitrary files in your Google Drive. Access tokens remain in Chrome's identity cache and are not stored by the extension.

What we do not collect

Job Application Tracker does not operate a server, analytics service, advertising service, telemetry endpoint, or cross-user database. It does not fill in or submit job applications on your behalf.

Your choices

You can delete drafts, disconnect Google, or change Google accounts from the extension. Disconnecting clears local drafts, queued records, the connected-sheet configuration, and local account data. You control retention of records already written to your Google Sheet.

Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the extension changes. Before public release, this page will include a support contact for privacy questions.
